4 Make the PDF & print

Before you print

  • Print at 100% / Actual size — not "Fit to page". Turn off any "scale to paper" setting.
  • Paper size: US Letter. For cardstock use the bypass / manual tray and pick a heavy or thick paper type in the printer driver.
  • Print one test page first and measure the 1 inch square with a ruler.
Sizing is only correct if the page is printed at 100%.
